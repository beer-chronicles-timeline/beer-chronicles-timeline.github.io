Two sisters gave this brewery its name. Meet The Sisters Brewery in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

Founded in Breukelen in 2015, the Dutch project grew out of homebrewing by Annamijn and Josefien and their father, Wilfran Beijeman. By August that year, Queen Bee IPA and Drone porter were already on sale, while the family was crowdfunding to expand the business. Production took place at De Fontein, bringing the family's beers to market through contract brewing.

Meet @the_sisters_brewery at the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.
