Good friends of Trier’s craft-beer scene, Camba Bavaria have a special place in this edition of The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

Their story began in 2008, when Markus Lohner founded Camba Bavaria in an old mill beside the Alz in Truchtlaching. It started as a working showroom for his brewery-equipment company, BrauKon. Customers could see the systems in action and taste the variety of beers they could produce. Those test brews helped the showroom grow into a brewery making both traditional Bavarian beers and international styles.

Meet @camba_bavaria at the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.
