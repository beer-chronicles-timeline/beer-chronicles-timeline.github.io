Like Blech.Brut, Atelier VRAI are among my personal NEIPA heroes. I love their beers and order almost every new release, so I’m especially happy to see them in this year’s festival line-up.

Atelier VRAI is next in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

Their story began in 2016, when Nico Döring founded Atelier der Braukünste, the company now known as Atelier VRAI. The starting point was a small nano-brewing system at Romrod Castle in Hesse.

Meet Atelier VRAI at the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.

Explore their story on Beer Chronicles through the link in the accompanying Story.
