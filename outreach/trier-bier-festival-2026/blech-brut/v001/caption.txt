I’m a real Blech.BRUT fan. In my opinion, they’re one of the best IPA/NEIPA breweries in Germany, and I order almost every new release. So I’m especially happy to see them in this year’s festival line-up.

Blech.BRUT is up first in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

Founded in Bamberg in 2018, Blech.BRUT has its own entry on the Beer Chronicles timeline.

Meet Blech.BRUT at the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.

Explore their story on Beer Chronicles through the link in the accompanying Story.
