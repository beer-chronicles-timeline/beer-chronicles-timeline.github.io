Blech.Brut Is Founded In Bamberg | Beer Chronicles Skip to main content BEER BEER CHRONICLES An Interactive Beer History Timeline CHRONICLES Beer History Entry Main content 2018 Blech.Brut Is Founded In Bamberg Breweries In 2018, Benedikt Steger founded Blech.Brut as a craft-beer project in Bamberg, one of Germany’s most historically important beer cities. Steger had previously brewed for craft breweries in Germany, London, and Paris before starting Blech.Brut as a gypsy brewer without its own physical brewery or taproom. Blech.Brut became one of the most visible modern German craft-beer names, especially for balanced dry-hopped lagers, intensely aromatic IPAs, and a strong can-focused visual identity. The brewery’s early production was closely associated with Brauhaus Binkert in the Bamberg region, but that chapter is now historical: Brauhaus Binkert closed in 2025. Current beer listings instead show a stronger production connection between Blech.Brut and Yankee & Kraut in Ingolstadt, linking two highly regarded modern German craft-beer names in the same broader scene.
