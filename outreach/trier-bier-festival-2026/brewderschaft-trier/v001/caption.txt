This one is personal: I’m one of the brewers in Brewderschaft Trier, our local homebrewing club. At this year’s festival, you’ll also find my own beer: Level 2 Monk, a Belgian Dubbel from Schmaidt's Nerd Brewing.

The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026 continues with a story close to home.

Founded in 2022, Brewderschaft Trier brings local homebrewers together to share ingredients and equipment, taste each other’s beers and exchange feedback. Proceeds from the group’s festival beers go to charitable causes.

Come and discover Brewderschaft Trier at the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.
