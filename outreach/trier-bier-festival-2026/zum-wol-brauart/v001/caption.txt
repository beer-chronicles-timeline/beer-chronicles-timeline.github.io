I’m especially happy to feature Zum WØL BrauArt: Laura and Lennart are good friends from the Saarbrücken area.

They founded Zum WØL BrauArt in Saarbrücken in 2021, bringing their passion for craft beer and design together during the pandemic. Modern international beer styles and creative branding have been part of the project from the start.

Their founding story is the next feature in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.
