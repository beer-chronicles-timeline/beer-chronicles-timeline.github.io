The people behind Kraft Bräu are good friends, and they’re also the hosts of the festival that brings this series together. I’m delighted to feature the brewery’s beginnings in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

On 28 September 1998, Kraft Bräu officially opened at Blesius Garten in Trier-Olewig. Hans-Werner Kraft and Klaus Tonkaboni established the house brewery, which launched with three unfiltered beers: a pale beer, a dark beer, and a wheat beer.
