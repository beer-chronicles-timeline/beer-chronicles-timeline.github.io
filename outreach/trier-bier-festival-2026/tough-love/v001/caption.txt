Tough Love started small: 16–40 litres at a time. Meet the Portuguese brewery in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

Its story began with a trip in 2017 that sparked an interest in craft beer. Back in Portugal, exploring unfamiliar styles led to new friendships, brewing courses, reading and experiments at home. Those early batches were brewed for personal enjoyment. They laid the groundwork for the later commercial project led by Vítor Fontes, with a brewery in Esmoriz, near Porto.

Meet Tough Love at the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.
