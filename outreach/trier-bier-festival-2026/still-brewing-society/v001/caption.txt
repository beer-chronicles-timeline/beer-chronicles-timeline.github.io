Thirty years of brewing experience, and a project of his own: meet Still Brewing Society, the next feature in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

On 7–8 August 2026, Still Brewing Society appeared at the Bingen Bier Festival in Bingen am Rhein. The organiser introduced it as Andi Dietrich’s own project, following his appearance with Brauquadrat the previous year. Behind the new name is a brewer with around three decades of experience across different breweries.

Now @still.brewing.society is on the lineup for the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.
