Still Brewing Society Appears at the Bingen Bier Festival

Events

Still Brewing Society appeared at the Bingen Bier Festival in Bingen am Rhein on August 7–8, 2026. The festival introduced it as Andi Dietrich’s own project, following his participation with Brauquadrat at the previous year’s event. The organizer described Dietrich as a brewer with about three decades of experience across different breweries. Still Brewing Society joined a lineup of German and international producers at the Rhine-side festival. Its appearance documents the new project’s public presence in 2026, with attendees recording beers such as All Day during the event.
