Craftprotz is a Trier beer institution for me. The people behind @craftprotz_kreativbierbar are very good friends, and it’s where #craftbeerszenetrier holds its regular Stammtisch. I’m delighted to feature the bar and its brewing project, TRVRS, in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

In 2020, during the pandemic, the project began barrel-aging experiments in Trier under the Craftprotz name. It adopted the name TRVRS in 2022, a reference to Treveris, the Latin name associated with Trier. The project’s beers are also served at Craftprotz.

Meet Craftprotz at the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.
