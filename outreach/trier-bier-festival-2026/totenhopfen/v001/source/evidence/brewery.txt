Majestic! 440ML Quick View Tshirt [Crow] - in 5 colors Quick View Snapback View All ABOUT TOTENHOPFEN BRAUHAUS Going from home to commercial brewing in 2017, Totenhopfen is set to shake up the burgeoning brewing scene in Luxembourg. With an impressive collection of awards, Totenhopfen has already established a presence in multiple European countries, such as Luxembourg, France, Germany, Belgium, The Netherlands, Portugal, Hungary, Austria, Bulgaria, Slovakia and Lithuania. With the goal of having its own Brewpub, Totenhopfen currently brews at different breweries throughout Europe, contributing to the European project of connecting people. At Totenhopfen, we firmly believe that Craft Beer enhances the quality of life. It's about having fun, getting to know people, sharing, socializing and constantly being challenged and surprised. ​ Totenhopfen's beers are renowned not only for their experimental and well-balanced flavors but also for their visually stunning labels.
