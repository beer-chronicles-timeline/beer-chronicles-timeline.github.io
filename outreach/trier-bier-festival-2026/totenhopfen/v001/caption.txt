Just across the border from Trier, Luxembourg has its own craft-beer stories. Totenhopfen is next in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

In 2017, Totenhopfen Brauhaus moved from homebrewing to commercial brewing. The Luxembourg project has brewed at different breweries across Europe, exploring modern styles and experimental flavours. It describes its cultural setting as between German and Belgian beer traditions.

Meet Totenhopfen at the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten. It’s a chance to discover beers from our Luxembourg neighbours right here in Trier.
