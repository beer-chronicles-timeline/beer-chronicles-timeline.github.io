This time, The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026 takes us to Mainz—close to Trier, and home to Schwarze Rose.

Their story began with a group of friends sharing an apartment in Bingen, known as Schwarze Rose. Discovering IPAs, stouts and sours together led them to develop recipes on small brewing systems. At the beginning of 2019, they took those experiments into commercial production through contract brewing at partner breweries in Germany.

Now @schwarzerose_craftbeer is joining the Trier Beer Festival for the first time. Meet them on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.

Explore the step from homebrewing to commercial brewing on Beer Chronicles through the link in the accompanying Story.
