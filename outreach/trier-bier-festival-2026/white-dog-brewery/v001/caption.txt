A white dog gave this Dutch brewery its name. Meet White Dog Brewery in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

In 2018, White Dog moved from homebrewing to commercial contract brewing after two years of brewing as a hobby. Based in Dordrecht and founded by Ricky Maertzendorff, it took its name from his West Highland White Terrier, Westie. White Dog developed its own recipes for production at other breweries, bringing its beers to market without a dedicated production brewery. Its later range grew to include hop-forward IPAs, fruit-heavy smoothie sours and pastry stouts.
