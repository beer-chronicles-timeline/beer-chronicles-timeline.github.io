Eremita’s commercial story began with a single beer.

This instalment of The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026 takes us to Birenbach in Baden-Württemberg. Benjamin Wezel started brewing in 2014 after watching a television documentary about beer. In 2016, he registered a brewing business and began selling his beer: the commercial beginnings of Eremita Braukunst.

He met Carola “Ola” Seibold in 2017, and she became his partner in life and brewing. Another milestone followed in 2022, when Eremita became certified organic.

Meet @eremitabraukunst at the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.
