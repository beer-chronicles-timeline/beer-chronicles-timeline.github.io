2016

Eremita Braukunst Begins Selling Beer

Breweries

In 2016, Benjamin Wezel registered a brewing business and began selling a single beer, marking the commercial beginnings of Eremita Braukunst in Birenbach, Baden-Württemberg. A precision mechanic by trade, he had started brewing in 2014 after watching a television documentary about beer. The move into sales turned his brewing experiments into a business. In 2017, he met Carola “Ola” Seibold, who became his partner in life and brewing. The small operation subsequently expanded beyond its original 50-liter brewing system, developing a range of beers made by hand.
