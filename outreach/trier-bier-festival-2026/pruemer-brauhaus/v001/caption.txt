From Prüm in the Eifel to Trier: I’m featuring Prümer Brauhaus in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

The Brauhaus opened in Prüm in 2021, combining beer brewed on the premises with a restaurant centred on seasonal and regional cooking. That August, it hosted the Jazz-Initiative Eifel’s first Prümer Nachkirmes, a series of benefit concerts for people affected by severe weather. It was an early connection between the new brewpub and local cultural life.

Prümer Brauhaus is joining the 12th Trier Beer Festival for the first time on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.
