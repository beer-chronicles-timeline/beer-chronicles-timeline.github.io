Brewmaster Henne is a great friend to Trier’s beer community, so I’m especially pleased to feature Schmelzer Brauhaus in The Stories Behind the Trier Beer Festival 2026.

Established in 2000 by the Dörr and Quinten families in Schmelz, Saarland, the Brauhaus combines a restaurant with a brewery making its own Erzbräu beer. With the brewhouse beside the dining area and beer garden, brewing is part of the experience on site.

Meet Schmelzer Brauhaus at the Trier Beer Festival on 2–3 October 2026 at Blesius Garten, Trier, hosted by @kraft_braeu_trier and @blesius_garten.

Explore their story on Beer Chronicles through the link in the accompanying Story.
